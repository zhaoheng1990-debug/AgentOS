# Experiment Retrospective v0.65-v0.89

## Audit Decision

The series was well governed at the artifact level but became patch-driven at
the theory level.

Preregistration, fresh holdouts, external panels, no-write boundaries, hashes,
and negative-result preservation generally worked. The methodological failure
was different: a failed proxy often generated the next schema field, witness,
stage, or compiler rule before an ObjectUpgradingAudit established the
ontology-level research object.

The corrective decision is:

`ROLL_BACK_ENGINEERING_AUTHORITY_TO_V0_65`

This is not a claim that v0.65 was a successful capability. It is the last
clean theory-driven evidence coordinate before the first long repair chain.
Later experiments remain immutable evidence and are used below to revise the
theory.

## Provenance Limitation Before v0.65

The CoreSlim `0.4.0-alpha.21` release commit imported a large prehistory of
local collective-cognition work in one Git commit. That archive contains
multiple nested version namespaces and cannot be honestly reconstructed as one
globally ordered v0.1-v0.63 commit chain.

The prehistory can be grouped, but not assigned a stronger per-round Git
provenance than the archive supplies:

| Evidence cluster | Main question | Preserved lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local model collaboration pilots | Can peers correct a small-model baseline? | Additional answers are not automatically useful; correction surplus and retained-correct performance must be separated. |
| Clarification and negative-evidence series | When should a role ask, defer, veto, or challenge? | Action labels need semantic grounding; generic veto and defer roles easily become conservative filters. |
| Cognitive-action and routing series | Can role selection reduce waste while preserving gain? | Routing needs object-conditional evidence and cannot rely on role names or historical credit alone. |
| Structure and problem-emergence series | Can a group expand object structure before solving? | Structure-first expansion is a plausible upstream object, but the archived tests do not establish autonomous problem emergence. |
| Portfolio, selection, and retention series | Can useful deltas survive, displace, and transfer? | Truth state, research value, lineage, and retention authority must remain distinct. |

These are retained as historical evidence clusters. A future archival project
may reconstruct them from manifests and receipts, but they do not authorize
new architecture in this reset.

## Cross-Round Facts

The following observations survive the rollback:

1. More Provider work can increase recall while reducing effective Cbit.
2. Typed decomposition improves auditability but does not by itself create
   independent cognitive contribution.
3. A role is not cognitively distinct merely because it has a different
   prompt or schema.
4. Evidence binding, semantic warrant, context utility, and final state utility
   are distinct objects.
5. Exact grounding is useful as a fail-closed provenance gate, but literal
   grounding is not a complete semantic criterion.
6. A downstream role can amplify an upstream framing error.
7. Abstention and fail-closed removal must be scored separately from valid
   correction.
8. External panel agreement is itself object-dependent and may expose ontology
   ambiguity rather than supply ground truth.
9. Token cost is not the primary objective, but additional cost needs positive
   marginal effective Cbit.
10. Fresh holdouts prevented post-hoc success claims, but repeated fresh tests
    cannot compensate for an under-specified theory.

## Rival Explanations Still Open

- The observed failures may arise from same-Provider error correlation rather
  than from multi-role organization in general.
- Some benchmark losses may be coordinate mismatch rather than factual error.
- The organization may need genuinely asymmetric evidence access, not merely
  context isolation.
- A coordinator may preserve complementary information, or may simply create
  another correlated bottleneck.
- The central missing object may be cognitive-object identification before
  evidence classification.

No archived experiment discriminates all of these rivals.

## ObjectUpgradingAudit

### Previous object

Improve benchmark receipt correctness by adding roles, witnesses, stages, and
compiler rules.

### Object level

Observable/proxy level. Receipt validity, rationale F1, label accuracy,
admission state, and mutation counts are readbacks, not collective-cognition
ontology.

### Higher-level object

The conditions under which an organization of bounded cognitive agents
produces positive marginal effective Cbit over its best member while
preserving object identity, evidence provenance, and error diversity.

### Why the lift is warranted

It explains multiple anomalies at once:

- why more roles often added no information;
- why same-Provider stages amplified a shared framing;
- why veto systems looked safer while becoming cognitively empty;
- why object binding helped interpretation without improving utility;
- why benchmark-specific fixes failed to transfer.

It also reduces the number of free architectural variables. Roles, receipts,
and stages are no longer presumed useful; each must prove unique conditional
contribution under the theory.

## Rollback Decision

The active engineering base is v0.65, commit
`af0cef20eaab9debf040b643c48a01995ba58e14`.

Why not v0.86? Its conceptual factorization is valuable, but the Git state also
contains the full v0.66-v0.85 additive module chain. v0.86 itself is explicitly
a benchmark entry, not a validated capability.

Why retain v0.86-v0.87 conceptually? They provide the clearest object split and
a valid negative test of one factor. Their conclusions enter the new theory as
evidence, not as inherited code.

The v0.89 branch and all intermediate branches remain the immutable experiment
archive. No history is deleted, rewritten, or reclassified as successful.
