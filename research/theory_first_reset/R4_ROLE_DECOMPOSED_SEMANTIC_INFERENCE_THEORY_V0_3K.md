## Inherited Evidence

R4 v0.3J established a bounded but mixed result:

- the factorized representation improved exact revalidation from 10/12 to
  12/12 in both rounds;
- `TransformStatus` was 12/12 in both rounds;
- `InformationEffect` was 11/12 and 12/12;
- the wider receipt reduced `SourceIdentity` to 10/12 in both rounds;
- evidence binding changed from 12/12 to 8/12 across factorized order;
- factorized action agreement was 9/12 versus legacy 12/12;
- one provenance error produced a harmful false combine;
- deterministic Runtime validation blocked missing witnesses and inconsistent
  status-effect pairs.

The observed failure is classified as:

```text
COGNITIVE_LOAD_INTERFERENCE_IN_WIDE_RECEIPT
+ PRESENTATION_SENSITIVE_WITNESS_BINDING
```

This classification is an inference, not yet a causal result. A wider schema,
different field grouping, repeated context, and role specialization have not
been separated experimentally.

## Role Definition

A role is not a persona label or a prompt costume.

For this theory:

```text
CognitiveRole_i
= ObjectView_i
+ EvidenceAccess_i
+ OutputContract_i
+ BlindnessBoundary_i
+ ResponsibilityBoundary_i
```

A role exists operationally only when:

1. its semantic object is explicit;
2. its output fields are bounded;
3. its evidence access is recorded;
4. it cannot read another role's output;
5. it cannot emit the final Runtime relation or action;
6. its receipt can be removed and scored independently.

Two isolated calls to the same Provider instantiate functional role
specialization. They do not establish independent priors or independent
cognitive subjects.

## Candidate Decomposition

### Provenance-State Role

Semantic responsibility:

- `SourceIdentity`;
- `LineageCoupling`;
- `TransformStatus`;
- provenance witness bundle.

Question:

```text
What is the origin and lineage relation, and is a transformation relation
verified, merely asserted, absent, or of unknown applicability?
```

### Effect-Scope Role

Semantic responsibility:

- `InformationEffect`;
- `AddedUncertainty`;
- `TargetClaimRelation`;
- effect-and-scope witness bundle.

Question:

```text
What informational change occurred, how much uncertainty was added, and which
claim scope is affected?
```

### Runtime Composer

Runtime:

- validates both schemas;
- validates case, evidence, and role binding;
- checks allowed `TransformStatus + InformationEffect` pairs;
- preserves missing and conflicting states;
- compiles relation, action, and revalidation deterministically;
- fails closed on incomplete or incoherent premises.

Runtime does not infer a missing semantic field or repair Provider evidence.

## Why These Boundaries

The provenance-state role groups attributes about origin, derivation, and the
epistemic status of the transformation relation.

The effect-scope role groups attributes about what information changed and the
claim boundary over which that change matters.

The grouping is accepted only as a falsifiable candidate. A cross-role
dependency may make it invalid. In particular, `TransformStatus` and
`InformationEffect` must remain jointly coherent after independent inference.

## Causal Contrast

The narrowest identifiable contrast is not:

```text
one wide call versus two narrow calls
```

That contrast confounds specialization with an extra context and extra sample.

The required matched contrast is:

```text
Wide paired control:
  wide call P -> all six attributes and both witness bundles
  wide call E -> all six attributes and both witness bundles
  consume provenance fields from P and effect-scope fields from E

Role-decomposed arm:
  provenance call -> only provenance-state fields and witnesses
  effect call -> only effect-scope fields and witnesses
  consume the corresponding fields from each call
```

Both arms therefore have:

- two isolated Provider contexts per round;
- identical public evidence;
- identical selected field source;
- identical final deterministic composer;
- identical private reference;
- no access to the other call's output;
- no final relation or action generated by Provider.

The manipulated variable is the unrelated semantic burden carried by each
source call.

`P` and `E` are frozen Runtime extraction positions, not role instructions
shown to the wide-arm Provider. Both wide prompts and schemas must be
identical. The Provider must not know which half of either wide receipt will be
consumed.

## Theoretical Readbacks

For role group `i`, define:

```text
E_i = exact label error
B_i = witness-binding error
D_i = forward-reverse disagreement
```

Define cross-burden interference:

```text
I_i =
  Error(wide source call fields consumed for role i)
  - Error(narrow role i)
```

Positive `I_i` indicates that asking the same context to carry unrelated
semantic responsibility damaged the consumed role packet.

Composition readbacks:

```text
C_pair = valid TransformStatus + InformationEffect pair rate
R_exact = exact revalidation trajectory
A_exact = exact Runtime action
H = harmful false combine or false deduplicate count
```

The result must remain a vector:

```text
(semantic exactness, witness binding, coherence, revalidation,
 action accuracy, harm, order drift, tokens, latency)
```

No unfrozen scalar may trade harmful certainty against token savings.

## Possibility Space

### M1 Responsibility-Bounded Interference

Unrelated semantic responsibility creates cross-attribute competition inside a
wide receipt. Removing that burden improves exact role packets and witness
binding while preserving cross-role coherence.

Expected signature:

- positive `I_i` in the previously affected provenance or witness dimensions;
- split-role revalidation remains at the factorized ceiling;
- split-role action accuracy is no worse than the matched wide control;
- zero harmful false combine and false deduplicate;
- forward-reverse agreement improves;
- token cost is equal or lower, but cost is not the primary pass condition.

### M0 No Decomposition Benefit

The v0.3J deviations were ordinary sampling or presentation drift. Matched
wide and split arms perform equivalently.

### M2 Context-Reset Benefit

Improvement comes from receiving two independent contexts, not from bounded
responsibility.

The two-call wide paired control directly tests this rival. If it matches the
split arm, role specialization has no identified effect.

### M3 Composition-Boundary Failure

Narrow roles improve their local fields, but independently correct-looking
packets create invalid or unsupported cross-role combinations.

Expected signature:

- local role accuracy improves;
- `C_pair`, relation, action, or revalidation does not;
- Runtime blocks rather than repairs the conflict.

This result would justify studying a semantic relation coordinator separately.
It would not justify adding one inside this experiment.

### M4 Global-Context Requirement

The transformation object cannot be split along the proposed boundary.
Removing the other field group degrades semantic accuracy or coherence.

Expected signature:

- one or both narrow roles perform worse than their matched wide source;
- status-effect inconsistency rises;
- missing cross-role assumptions explain the errors.

### M5 Presentation Drift

Arm or case order explains the observed differences.

Expected signature:

- gains reverse or disappear under counterbalanced order;
- within-arm disagreement remains high;
- evidence binding changes without a stable role-specific direction.

## Discriminating Predictions

The leading model requires all of the following on one new sixteen-case corpus
in two counterbalanced rounds:

1. split exact role-bundle accuracy exceeds the matched wide control by at
   least 2/32 case-role bundles in both rounds;
2. no split semantic attribute is below 14/16 in either round;
3. split witness-bundle exactness is not lower than the matched wide control in
   either role or round;
4. split revalidation is 16/16 in both rounds;
5. split Runtime action accuracy is at least 15/16 in both rounds and is not
   lower than the matched wide control;
6. split forward-reverse Runtime action agreement is at least 15/16;
7. no false combine or false deduplicate occurs;
8. all incomplete or incoherent receipts fail closed;
9. the same deterministic replay is byte-identical;
10. total Provider use stays within the frozen call and token budget.

M1 is rejected as stated if any leading-model requirement fails.

The result is `INCONCLUSIVE_CONSTRUCTION_FAILURE`, not an M1 rejection, if
either arm receives unequal evidence, an unequal number of source contexts,
different compiler logic, leaked private references, or a repaired semantic
contract after exposure.

## Proxy Validity Limits

Exact synthetic labels and witnesses are observable proxies for bounded
semantic-role performance. They do not measure ontological Cbit directly.

The experiment can identify whether narrowing a receipt improves these frozen
readbacks under one Provider and one synthetic task family. It cannot identify
whether:

- role contexts hold genuinely independent information;
- the packets have positive `J_i^uniq` beyond a serial matched control;
- a coordinator captures organizational information;
- the same boundary transfers to open-world evidence;
- a role can define new cognitive objects or research questions.

## Experimental Operator

The smallest admissible Provider experiment uses:

- sixteen fresh cases;
- four provenance-edge cases;
- four effect-scope-edge cases;
- four cross-role interaction cases;
- four null or unknown-applicability cases;
- exact private role labels and role-specific witness bundles;
- two calls per arm per round;
- two counterbalanced rounds;
- eight logical calls total;
- no retries except a preregistered mechanical retry;
- no Provider semantic coordinator.

The corpus must not reuse a v0.3J labeled case record, case text, or case
identifier. The frozen ontology enum values necessarily remain shared across
versions.

A formal zero-Provider test is insufficient because v0.3I already established
compiler coherence. The unresolved object is Provider inference under matched
semantic burden.

## Cbit And Cost

Expected theory gain:

```text
wide-schema interference
  versus context-reset benefit
  versus composition-boundary failure
  versus global-context requirement
```

This comparison has positive theoretical Cbit because each outcome changes the
next architecture decision:

- M1 supported -> bounded role contracts become candidates;
- M0 or M2 supported -> do not add role modules;
- M3 supported -> study semantic coordination as a separate object;
- M4 supported -> revise the proposed object boundary;
- M5 supported -> improve presentation robustness before architecture work.

Token and latency are Pareto coordinates. A stable semantic and safety gain may
justify higher cost. Cost alone cannot convert a cognitively negative result
into a pass.

Provisional ceiling for preregistration:

- eight logical calls;
- at most sixteen physical attempts;
- at most 100000 total tokens.
