# Science institutional constraints v0.26

Source: James Evans, Benjamin Bratton, and Blaise Aguera y Arcas,
"Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion," *Science*,
DOI `10.1126/science.aeg1895`.

This source is used as an architectural direction, not as benchmark evidence.
The v0.26 experiment freezes six upper-level constraints:

1. Cognition may be plural and relational, but one Provider call is not evidence
   of group cognition.
2. Structured disagreement must preserve counterevidence and support explicit
   reverse, object-switch, and coordinate-switch operations.
3. Perspectives must be bound to positions in an object-relation graph. Merely
   assigning role names does not create independent cognitive agents.
4. Frozen inputs, receipts, replay, rollback, and authority boundaries are part
   of the cognitive runtime.
5. Recursive sub-society formation remains out of scope until structure-first
   problem emergence demonstrates positive gain.
6. Provider output is cognitive support. Runtime retains evidence scope,
   validation, scoring, state transition, and final gate authority.

All v0.26 outputs are synthetic, candidate-only, and external to CoreSlim.
They have no selection, retention, baseline, or production authority.
