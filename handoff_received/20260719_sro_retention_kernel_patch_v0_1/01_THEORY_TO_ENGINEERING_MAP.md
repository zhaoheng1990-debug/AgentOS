## Key object lift

The existing CoreSlim retention gate is a storage-admission scalar. The series
identified a different missing object: query-conditioned reuse routing.

```text
FailureMode
  exact/surface/current-state/unconditional reuse fails under collision/drift

MissingObject
  calibrated witness-task compatibility with uncertainty and validity state

ObjectLift
  GradedSROCompatibilityGate

Proxy
  provider/matcher route-probability receipt

Test
  direct/local/observe/reject/revise/abstain paths, malformed receipt,
  drift, collision, evidence-scope downgrade and delayed reveal ordering
```

## Evidence ceiling encoded in the patch

Synthetic/formal evidence may support local reconstruction or shadow use, but
it cannot authorize `DIRECT_REUSE`. Direct reuse requires project-scoped or
externally validated evidence, a frozen calibration receipt, sufficient trace,
current validity, low uncertainty and low drift.
